<p class="home-intro">I am a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science and Technology at the
<strong>School of Computer Science and Engineering, Nanjing University of Science and
Technology (NJUST)</strong>, advised by <strong>Prof. Haofeng Zhang</strong>. I study how
visual models can generalize when semantic structure and visual evidence no longer line
up.</p>
